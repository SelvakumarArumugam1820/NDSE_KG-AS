Overview

What is a Land Survey?

A land survey is the scientific process of precisely determining the terrestrial or three-dimensional positions of points and the distances and angles between them. It establishes exact boundaries, area measurements, and spatial relationships of land parcels using calibrated instruments and established methodologies.

Why is it Required?

Land surveys are legally mandated for property transactions, registration, construction permits, and dispute resolution. Without a certified survey, land ownership cannot be accurately established. It prevents encroachments, costly boundary disputes, and ensures compliance with local regulatory authorities.

Who Needs it?

Land owners, real estate developers, builders, government agencies, infrastructure companies, agricultural landholders, and corporate entities acquiring land for industrial or commercial purposes all require professional land survey services.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Land Survey?

A land survey precisely measures and maps the boundaries, area, and physical features of a land parcel using scientific instruments and established methodologies.

Why is a Land Survey required before property purchase?

A survey confirms exact boundaries, area, and encumbrances before purchase, protecting you from legal disputes and ensuring what you buy matches what the seller claims.

How long does a Land Survey take?

Most surveys are completed in 1-3 days on-site, followed by 1-2 days for data processing, drawing preparation, and certified report generation.

What instruments do you use?

We use calibrated Total Stations, Differential GPS (DGPS), Auto Levels, and advanced AutoCAD/GIS software to deliver millimetre-accurate results.

Is the survey report accepted by the government?

Yes. All our survey reports are prepared by licensed surveyors and are legally accepted by revenue departments, registration offices, courts, and planning authorities.
