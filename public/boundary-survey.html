Overview

What is a Boundary Survey?

A boundary survey is the precise field determination of the legal property lines of a parcel of land. It identifies, locates, and physically marks the corners and boundary lines of land ownership, resolving ambiguities in historical records and on-ground features.

Why is it Required?

Without defined boundaries, property ownership is perpetually at risk. Boundary surveys are essential before constructing walls, fences, or buildings near property edges. They are also required for legal registration, court submissions, and before any real estate transaction to prevent future encroachment disputes.

Who Needs it?

Individual land owners, housing societies, residential builders, commercial developers, government departments, and any organization acquiring or improving property where boundary clarity is required.

Key Benefits

Accurate Measurements — Centimetre-level boundary precision using DGPS
Legal Compliance — Reports accepted by revenue offices and courts
Faster Project Execution — Clear boundaries enable immediate construction planning
Reduced Construction Errors — Prevents building on wrong parcels or encroaching neighbours
Government Approval Support — All drawings prepared for DTCP and local authority submission

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Boundary Survey?

A boundary survey accurately locates and marks the legal boundaries of a property using field measurements and official records.

When is a Boundary Survey required?

It is needed before buying or selling land, constructing a fence or wall, resolving neighbour disputes, or applying for building permits.

How are boundaries physically marked?

We place permanent iron rods, concrete pillars, or GPS-verified monuments at all boundary corners after precise measurement.

Can the survey resolve encroachment issues?

Yes. A certified boundary survey with legal coordinates provides evidence accepted by courts and revenue departments to resolve encroachment disputes.

How long does a Boundary Survey take?

Most boundary surveys are completed within 1-2 days in the field, with reports delivered within 3-5 working days.
