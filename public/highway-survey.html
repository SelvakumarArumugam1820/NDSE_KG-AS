Overview

What is a Highway Survey?

A highway survey is a large-scale route survey specifically tailored for major arterial roads, national highways (NH), and state highways (SH). It requires establishing a highly accurate DGPS control network over long distances, detailed topographic mapping of a wide corridor, and precise levelling for pavement and drainage design.

Why is it Required?

Highways involve immense capital investment and strict design standards (NHAI/MoRTH). Survey data must be flawless to design grade separators, interchanges, bridges, and ensure safety at high speeds. It also identifies land acquisition limits and utility relocation requirements.

Who Needs it?

National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), State Highway Departments, concessionaires, EPC contractors, and highway design consultants.

Key Benefits

MoRTH Compliance — Surveys conducted per national highway standards
Detailed Infrastructure Mapping — Accurate data for bridges, culverts, and interchanges
Seamless Control Network — Continuous accuracy over hundreds of kilometers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle large highway projects?

Yes, we have the capacity and equipment to survey highway corridors spanning hundreds of kilometers.

What is an inventory survey?

It is the detailed recording of existing road conditions, including pavement type, width, condition of structures (bridges/culverts), and roadside furniture.
