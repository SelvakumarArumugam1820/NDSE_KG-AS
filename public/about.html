About NDSE

Nathan Digital Survey Engineers — digital survey engineering for land, construction and infrastructure.

Who we are

Nathan Digital Survey Engineers (NDSE) is a survey engineering company based in Tamil Nadu, with offices in Coimbatore and Manapparai, Trichy. We provide digital survey and mapping services and digital setting out (marking) services for landowners, developers, contractors and infrastructure projects.

Our work covers boundaries, topography, contours, water bodies, ground investigation, infrastructure, routes, GIS and LiDAR mapping, earthwork quantities and approval drawings — and the accurate marking of approved designs on the ground.

Our mission is to provide accurate and reliable surveying solutions using modern technology, so that clients can plan, approve and build with confidence.

Equipment & Technology

Our surveying equipment

Harnessing Modern Digital Technology. Experience the next generation of GIS, LiDAR, and Drone surveying technology with NDSE's digital survey division. Digital Survey Page

Total Station

Electronic measurement of angles and distances for boundary, topographical and construction work.

DGPS / GNSS

Satellite positioning for control points, boundary marking and large-area surveys.

Drone

Aerial data capture for mapping large or difficult-to-access sites.

LiDAR

Laser pulses that create detailed 3D point clouds, DTM, DSM and DEM outputs.

3D Scanner

Detailed capture of buildings and structures for as-built records.

Auto Level & Digital Level

Transferring heights from a known benchmark for level marking.
