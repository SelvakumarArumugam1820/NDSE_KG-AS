Frequently Asked Questions

What does a structural audit entail?

A structural audit involves non-destructive testing, visual inspections, and analytical modeling to verify the integrity and safety of an existing building.

Do you validate land titles?

Yes, our document verification survey includes a thorough examination of land titles, encumbrance certificates, and historical ownership records.
